# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

This privacy policy explains how the following apps, developed and published on Google Play by Carles Rojas under the name **Pinya** ("we", "us"), handle user information:

| App | Package name |
| --- | --- |
| Escape | com.PinyaGames.Escape |
| Let's Drive | com.PinyaGames.LetsDrive |
| Fanfastic | app.netlify.fanfastic |
| SimulChess | app.pinya.simulchess |

By using any of these apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Summary

- We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
- No account or sign-up is required.
- The apps contain no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## Information we collect

**None.** These apps do not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. They do not use the Android Advertising ID.

## Information stored on your device

Some apps may save settings or progress (for example, game progress or preferences) locally on your device so the app works as expected. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Device permissions

The apps do not request access to sensitive device features or data, such as your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

## Network access

Some apps may connect to the internet to load their content (for example, Fanfastic is delivered from a web host). In that case, the hosting provider may automatically process standard technical information needed to deliver the content, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not combine it with any other data.

## Third-party services

We do not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The apps are distributed through Google Play, which is governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Sharing and selling of data

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not share or sell it to anyone.

## Data security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted to us, there is no personal data on our side to protect or to be exposed in a breach.

## Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any user data. Any locally stored data can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## Children's privacy

These apps can be used by people of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete, but you can contact us with any question about these rights.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review it periodically.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at [apps@carlesrojas.com](mailto:apps@carlesrojas.com).
